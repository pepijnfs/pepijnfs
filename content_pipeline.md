# @vagabliss - Content Pipeline

How the content gets made, tested, and promoted. Five stages: Capture, Library, Assemble,
Test, Promote. The point is to make footage reusable, test one thing at a time, and only put
proven reels on the grid.

```
Capture  ->  Library  ->  Assemble  ->  Test (trial reels)  ->  Promote winners  ->  Repurpose
 shoot        organize     build the     cold-audience test       graduate to grid    carousel,
 the list     clips        master reel   read 24h insights        share to everyone   stories
```

Reality check on the layers (from how Instagram works):
- The **video and baked text** are made here / in an editor.
- The **trending audio** is added inside Instagram at post time. It cannot be baked or automated.
- So the pipeline produces finished-looking video variants; the audio and the actual posting are
  the manual, in-app last step.

---

## Stage 1: Capture (batch shoot)

Shoot for the edit you will make later, not single clips. Per location, get 8 to 12 clips
covering these types so the edit has variety:

| Type | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | the single most striking shot | opens the reel |
| Wide / establishing | the whole scene | sets the place |
| Detail | texture: food, water, a door, flowers | contrast to the wide |
| POV / motion | walking in, a hand, a boat | energy and immersion |
| The stay | room, terrace, breakfast, the view | the differentiator |
| Human | you in frame, walking away | personal brand |
| Closer | a lingering shot that can loop | end + rewatch |

Rules: shoot in golden hour, move slow, hold each shot 5+ seconds. Overshoot.

---

## Stage 2: Library (organize so clips are mixable)

Name and sort so any clip can be found and recombined. Suggested structure:
```
library/
  gargnano/
    hook_jetty.mp4
    wide_lakefront.mp4
    detail_flowers.mp4
    stay_terrace.mp4
    human_walkaway.mp4
  gubbio/
    ...
hooks.txt          # the text-hook lines
tracks/            # your own / licensed audio only
```
Tag by location and shot type. This library is what feeds both manual edits and the
reel_variant_generator.

---

## Stage 3: Assemble (shot order inside a reel)

The 4-act structure, about 15 to 20 seconds:

| Act | Time | Shot | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0 to 2s | your strongest shot, text hook on it | hold long enough to read the text |
| Build | 2 to 10s | wide, then detail, then POV | alternate wide and tight for variety |
| Stay / payoff | 10 to 15s | the stay or the value shot | where completion is highest |
| Close | 15 to 20s | lingering or human shot | hold longer, loop back to the hook |

Sequencing rules the pros use:
- Alternate wide and tight so no two shots in a row look the same. Sameness loses retention.
- Save one "wow" for the middle and one for the end.
- Make the last shot flow into the first so a rewatch is seamless. Rewatches help distribution.
- Put the value and the CTA near the end, where the people still watching are the warmest.

---

## Stage 4: Test (trial reels, one variable at a time)

Trial reels go to non-followers, so they are a free test lab. The discipline: change **one
variable per batch** so you can tell what actually worked.

Variables, in order of leverage:
1. Hook text (biggest lever) - test 3 to 5 hooks on the same edit
2. Opening shot - same hook, 2 different first shots
3. Length - 10s vs 20s
4. Audio - trending vs original (added in-app)
5. Cover frame

What to read after 24 hours, in priority order: **sends, then saves, then retention (watch
time), then follows.** Likes are the weakest signal, mostly ignore them.

Example batch: pick the Gubbio edit, run 4 trial variants that differ only in the hook line.
Whichever gets the most sends and saves is the hook you use going forward.

---

## Stage 5: Promote and repurpose

- **Winner:** tap Share to Everyone so it hits your grid, and reuse its hook formula on the
  next reel.
- **Loser:** leave it as a trial (it does not clutter the grid) and note why it missed.
- **Repurpose the winner:** turn it into a carousel (the itinerary as slides), a story, and a
  reusable template. One winning idea should produce several posts.

Keep a simple log:
```
date | reel | variable tested | sends | saves | retention | verdict
```

---

## Posting cadence

- 3 to 4 reels a week, sustainable beats daily burnout.
- Rotate the pillars: The Find, The Stay, The Area, The Practical.
- Anchor one weekly series so people know what to come back for.
- Batch a shoot into a week or more of posts so you never miss a day.

---

## The loop

Capture once, cut a master, test hooks on trial reels, promote the winner, repurpose it, log
what worked, feed that back into the next hook list. The account improves because every post
teaches the next one, not because any single reel is perfect.
