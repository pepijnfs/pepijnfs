## Part 1 — The Instagram Reels algorithm in 2026 (what's actually known)

The honest headline: **the technical/craft advice is rock-solid, but most "hard" algorithm
numbers floating around are unverifiable.** In our pass, *every* specific ranking-signal
threshold we tested got refuted (0-3). So here's the split:

**Directionally confirmed (⚠️ reported — Mosseri statements, widely cited but not primary-verified in our pass):**
- Instagram head Adam Mosseri has repeatedly named three signals that matter most:
  **watch time, likes-per-reach, and sends-per-reach.** For Reels, **"sends" (sharing to a
  friend via DM) is the most heavily weighted distribution signal** — it tells the algorithm
  the content is worth spreading.
- **Saves** also signal durable value (a "keep this" vote).
- **Originality is rewarded, recycled content is penalised.** Native uploads shot/edited for
  Instagram outrank reposts; **visible TikTok watermarks and aggregated/recycled content get
  reach-throttled.**
- **Hashtags have shifted from growth levers to "filing labels."** Discovery is increasingly
  driven by **keywords in captions, on-screen text, and your profile (SEO)** rather than hashtags.
- **Smaller accounts (<50k) have a genuine organic-reach advantage** right now — good news for beginners.

**What FAILED verification (⚠️ do NOT treat as fact):**
- ❌ "60% retention baseline / 80%+ gets amplified" — killed 0-3.
- ❌ "30-40% skip rate is healthy, >50% kills reach" — killed 0-3.
- ❌ "The first 3 seconds *determine* performance" as a hard rule — killed 0-3.
- ❌ "Meta officially prescribes hook-in-first-frame + text-from-frame-one" — killed 0-3.
- ❌ "Question / contradiction / result hooks consistently outperform" — killed 0-3.

**Practical takeaway:** Optimise for **shares and saves** (the signals that are actually
confirmed) and for **completion** (people watching to the end). Ignore the precise
percentage thresholds — they're invented. Build content people *send to a friend*.

## Part 2 — Filming & cinematography (this is the strongest, best-verified section)

- ✅ **Shoot & export 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, MP4/H.264.** Keep your key subject in the
  **upper two-thirds** of the frame — the bottom ~290px is a UI safe zone covered by the
  caption and the like/comment/share buttons.
- ✅ **Bitrate beats resolution.** A **high-bitrate 4K capture (50 Mbps+)** looks better than a
  higher-resolution-but-low-bitrate file. *"A 4K video at low bitrate can look worse than 720p
  at high bitrate."* Shoot the highest-bitrate 4K your phone offers.
- ✅ **180° shutter rule for cinematic motion blur:** set shutter ≈ **2× your frame rate** —
  1/50s at 24fps, 1/60s at 30fps, **1/120s at 60fps.** Too-fast a shutter makes motion look
  stuttery and "security-cam."
- ✅ **Slow-mo silently drops resolution.** A phone that does 4K/60 may only manage 1080p (or
  720p) at 240fps. **Check your device's true slow-mo spec** before planning hero slow-motion shots.
- ✅ **DIY stabilisation works on a budget:** rubber-band your phone to any tripod and grip the
  tripod legs *below* the centre of gravity — instant makeshift glidecam. (A €100 gimbal is
  better, but this is free.)
- **Essential shot types to capture at every location** (standard cinematography vocabulary):
  **establishing/wide** (sets the place), **medium**, **detail/close-up** (texture: food,
  hands, water), **POV** (viewer's-eye immersion), and **reveal** (pan/tilt/step-out that
  unveils the view). Build every sequence from wide → detail → POV b-roll.
- **Golden hour** (first/last hour of light): low ISO (100-400), manual white balance ~5000-6000K
  to keep the warm tones, backlight your subject for rim light. This is the single biggest
  "expensive-looking" lever for travel footage.

## Part 3 — Editing & content creation

- ✅ **Retention rests on three levers:** (1) a **strong hook in the first ~1.5 seconds**,
  (2) a **visual change every 2-3 seconds** (new shot, push-in, text reveal), and (3) **captions
  for sound-off viewing** (most Reels are watched muted; captioned Reels get meaningfully more watch time).
- 🟡 **Length is content-dependent, not one-size:** **7-15s** for quick/entertainment/scenic
  hits, **15-30s** for storytelling/tutorials. 30-60s is the *most commonly posted* length but
  that's popularity, not proof it's optimal. Match length to how much story you actually have.
- 🟡 **~80% completion = excellent.** Rule of thumb: ~12s average watch on a 15s Reel is great.
  (Soft heuristic, not an official metric — but a fine internal target.)
- **Music & licensing (matters a lot for you — a resort promo is commercial):** ⚠️ Instagram's
  full music library is licensed for **personal/non-commercial** accounts only. **Business/brand
  accounts are limited to the Meta Sound Collection or properly-licensed original audio.** If
  you're posting for a hotel/brand, using a trending pop song can get the audio muted or the reel
  taken down. Anchor a recognisable *series* to original/licensed audio; ride trending sounds only
  on personal posts.
- **CTAs:** give viewers one clear next action (save this, comment a keyword, follow for the series).

## Part 4 — Travel-niche strategy

- **Niche down hard.** "Travel" is too broad to grow. Micro-niches grow faster and monetise
  better: *luxury/unique stays, budget solo travel, eco/slow travel, adventure, family travel,
  food-led travel.* Your reference reel points at a natural lane: **"unique alpine & design-led
  stays in Europe."**
- **Use a repeatable destination-reel structure:** **Hook → Place reveal → Experience → CTA**
  (target 15-30s). It's inherently save- and share-worthy because it answers "should I go here?"
- **Make it save-worthy** (people bookmark things they plan to *do*): name the place, the price,
  the season, how to book. Aesthetics alone get likes; *utility* gets saves and sends.
- **Batch on location.** Build a shot list before you arrive; capture your planned "hero" shots
  in one golden-hour window, then grab candid b-roll around it. One good shoot day = a week+ of
  posts. Keep a backlog so you never miss a posting day.

## Part 5 — Getting started & growth

- **Switch to a Creator account** (analytics + reach tools).
- **Profile = a landing page:** face-forward profile photo (trust), a bio that states your
  niche + personality + one CTA, and **keywords in your name/bio field** (SEO now drives discovery).
- **Consistency > perfection.** Post volume matters most early; a sustainable **3-4 Reels/week**
  beats a daily burnout. Batch so you can hold the cadence.
- **Engage as a creator, not a broadcaster** — reply to comments/DMs; sends and saves are the
  signals that actually move reach.
- **Start pitching early.** You don't need 100k. Creators land hosted stays and small deals at
  **2-3k engaged followers** in a clear niche.

## Part 6 — Monetization pathways for travel creators

- **Hotel / tourism-board partnerships** (the big one for your niche). Rough 2026 rate bands
  (⚠️ reported, varies wildly by market): *nano (1k-10k)* → complimentary stays worth ~$150-400/night
  for 2-3 posts; *micro (10k-100k)* → a stay **plus** ~$200-2,000 cash; *mid (100k-500k)* →
  ~$5,000-15,000 hosted trips.
- **Affiliate booking links** — join now, no follower minimum: **Booking.com (4-15%), Airbnb,
  Expedia (3-12%), Viator (8-15%), Stay22, Skyscanner.** Put a link aggregator in bio.
- **UGC (underrated for beginners):** you can **sell clips to hotels/airlines to use on *their*
  channels** — you get paid even when the content never posts to your own feed. Your reel-analysis
  toolkit + this teardown skill is literally a UGC service you could sell today.

## Part 7 — Teardown of your reference reel 🎬

The Austrian alpine-resort reel: **12.5s · 720×1280 · 30fps · H.264 · 8 hard-cut shots ~1.5s each,
cut on the beat (~77 BPM) · hook text "This stay in Austria >>" · scenic b-roll (natural ponds,
chalets, golden-hour hot-tub terrace, grazing sheep, mountain peaks).**

| Element | Verdict | Against best practice |
|---|---|---|
| **Native vertical 9:16, H.264** | ✅ Nails it | Correct format — but only **720p**, see below |
| **Beat-synced ~1.5s cuts** | ✅ Textbook | "Visual change every 2-3s" — this is *tighter*, keeps eyes locked |
| **Hook text from frame 1** | 🟡 Good, improvable | Text-on-screen is right; *"This stay in Austria >>"* is vague. Name the payoff. |
| **Scenic golden-hour b-roll** | ✅ Strong | Establishing + detail shots, warm light — the expensive-looking lever |
| **Resolution / fps** | ⚠️ Under-spec | **720p/30fps** leaves quality on the table — shoot **4K, high-bitrate**, 60fps for hero slow-mo |
| **Length** | 🟡 Defensible | 12.5s sits in the 7-15s "quick scenic" band; fine, but a 15-25s cut could tell a *story* (arrive → experience → CTA) |
| **Human / POV element** | ❌ Missing | All landscape, no face/POV. A person in-frame builds trust & the para-social pull that grows a *personal* brand |
| **Explicit CTA** | ❌ Missing | No "save this / where to book / follow for more." Add one — that's what earns saves & sends |
| **Music licensing** | ⚠️ Watch it | If this is a paid resort promo, the audio must be Meta Sound Collection / licensed, not a trending pop track |

**One-line summary:** the reel is a beautifully-paced *aesthetic* piece that would perform as
eye-candy, but it's built for **likes**, not for the **sends, saves, and follows** that actually
compound growth. Fix: sharper hook, a human moment, a clear CTA, and shoot it in 4K.

**A stronger recut of the same footage:**
1. **0.0-1.5s** — best single shot (the infinity-pond-with-mountains) + specific hook text:
   *"You can swim in this pond at 1,000m in Austria 🇦🇹"*
2. **1.5-12s** — keep the beat-synced montage (it's the strongest part).
3. **New 12-18s** — add a POV/human beat (someone stepping into the hot tub at golden hour).
4. **18-22s** — end card CTA: *"Save this for your Austria trip · full guide in comments."*

## Part 8 — Your 90-day getting-started action plan

**Weeks 1-2 — Foundation.** Pick the micro-niche (suggest: *unique alpine/design stays in Europe*).
Creator account. Rewrite bio with niche + keywords + one CTA + face photo. Study 20 top reels in
the niche; save the hooks that work.

**Weeks 3-6 — Build the engine.** Learn your phone's 4K/high-bitrate + 60fps settings. Do **one
batch shoot** with a shot list (wide/detail/POV/reveal at golden hour). Ship **3-4 Reels/week**,
each with a specific hook, captions, and one CTA. Use the `reel_analyzer` tool on your own posts
*and* on competitors' viral reels to reverse-engineer pacing.

**Weeks 7-10 — Iterate on data.** In Instagram Insights, watch **retention graphs, saves, and
sends** (not vanity likes). Double down on the hook style and length that hold attention. Start a
named, repeatable **series** anchored to one licensed audio track.

**Weeks 11-13 — Monetize.** Join 2-3 affiliate programs (Booking, Stay22, Viator). Pitch **5 local
hotels** a hosted-stay-for-content deal, even at a few thousand followers. Offer **UGC** (clips for
their channels) as a paid entry product.
