- **Optimus Asset Management** (South Korea, $1.3B) — Fake government-receivable funds sold to 3,000 investors; political ties. Under-covered outside Korea.
- **Wakon crypto Ponzi** (South Korea, $1.1B) — 'Casino-AI' platform promising 30% in 40 days; recent (2023-24). Fresh but check it isn't pure hype.
- **Korea CFD stock manipulation** (South Korea, $500M) — 2023 SG Securities crash; 25 convicted in 2025 in Korea's biggest manipulation case. Complex but timely.
- **Zhongzhi shadow-bank collapse** (China, $64B hole) — Shadow-banking giant declared insolvent 2023-24; Ponzi-like structure. Huge but diffuse protagonist.
- **PlusToken crypto Ponzi** (China, $3-6B) — One of the largest crypto Ponzis ever; moved markets when the coins were dumped. Under-covered vs its scale.
- **Kingold fake gold-bar loans** (China, $2.8B) — Wuhan jeweller pledged 83 tonnes of gilded-copper 'gold' as loan collateral. Incredible visual hook, tight protagonist (Jia Zhihong), evergreen.
- **Truong My Lan / Van Thinh Phat** (Vietnam, $27B) — Richest-woman-to-death-row arc; death sentence upheld unless she repays $9B. Largest bank fraud in history; ongoing 2025-26. (Asian, not China/Korea.)
- **Lai Xiaomin / Huarong** (China, $260M bribes) — State-asset-manager boss EXECUTED in 2021; three tonnes of cash in an apartment ('supermarket'). Rare death-penalty finance story.
- **Daewoo Group collapse** (South Korea, $43B) — Founder Kim Woo-choong inflated books by ~$19B, fled abroad for years. Once the world's biggest bankruptcy. Classic, evergreen, under-covered.
