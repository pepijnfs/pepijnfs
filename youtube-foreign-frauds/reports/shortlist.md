# Foreign Frauds — recent-fraud research shortlist

Snapshot: 2026-07-06. Method: web discovery → fact-check → YouTube saturation probe
(`youtube_saturation.py`). Ranked by fit to the channel DNA: **under-highlighted Asian
(China/Korea-weighted) financial crime, big scale, strong protagonist, evergreen** — not
hype-driven. "Saturation" = the biggest existing YouTube treatment; low = open lane.

> Reading the saturation score: it's a coarse signal. Some topics score "high opportunity"
> only because the sole existing videos are foreign-language news clips; a couple of "medium"
> scores come from *tangential* videos, not a real documentary on the case. Notes below
> correct for that by hand.

## Recommended top 5

### 1. Ezubao — China · $7.6B P2P Ponzi · ⭐ best overall fit
- **Story:** Ding Ning fabricated ~95% of investment projects, defrauded **900,000** people. Spent wildly (a reported $20M+ villa gifted to an executive, cars, Singapore property). When it collapsed, staff **buried 1,200+ account books in 80 bags six metres underground** — police needed excavators to dig them out. Ding got life.
- **Saturation:** essentially **zero** serious English coverage (top result ~300 views). Wide open.
- **Evergreen & on-brand:** timeless greed-and-collapse arc; a built-in $7.6B title number.
- **Title angle:** *"$7 Billion Buried in 80 Bags — China's Biggest Ponzi."*

### 2. Kingold — China · $2.8B fake gold · ⭐ best visual hook
- **Story:** Wuhan jeweller pledged **83 tonnes of gilded copper** as "gold" collateral for ~$2.8B in loans. Chairman **Jia Zhihong** (ex-military). Fooled banks and insurers for years; unravelled in 2020 when a default forced lenders to cut open the bars. Jia detained/charged 2021.
- **Saturation:** no definitive Kingold documentary — the top hit is a *tangential* "is China's gold fake?" video (264K). The case itself is open.
- **On-brand:** mirrors your existing titles (fake-cash, fake-sales). *"China's $2.8 Billion Fake Gold Scandal."* Unbeatable visuals.

### 3. Truong My Lan / Van Thinh Phat — Vietnam · $27B · ⭐ biggest scale
- **Story:** Looted Saigon Commercial Bank via thousands of shell-company loans — the **largest bank fraud in history**. Richest-woman-to-condemned arc.
- **⚠️ Fact update:** her death sentence is being **commuted to life** — Vietnam abolished the death penalty for embezzlement (June 2025); she must still repay ~$11B. Frame as "the $27B queen," not "facing execution."
- **Region caveat:** Asian but **not** China/Korea — flagging since your DNA is China/Korea. Include only if you're comfortable widening to SE Asia.
- **Saturation:** news clips (~250K) but no definitive long-form doc. Open. Some 2025-26 recency heat.

### 4. Lai Xiaomin / Huarong — China · executed · ⭐ best downfall drama
- **Story:** Boss of state asset-manager Huarong; investigators found **~3 tonnes of cash (~$260M) in an apartment he nicknamed "the supermarket,"** plus 100+ properties and mistresses. **Executed in January 2021** — a rare, shocking finance death-penalty case.
- **Saturation:** more covered — China Uncensored's version has ~1.6M — but from a political angle; a straight true-crime/finance retelling still has room.
- **Evergreen:** absolutely. The cash-apartment + execution hook is elite.

### 5. Zhongzhi — China · $64B hole · wildcard (biggest number, hardest to tell)
- **Story:** Shadow-banking giant, Ponzi-like structure, declared **insolvent** 2023-24 with a ~$64B shortfall. Barely covered (top ~1K views).
- **Weakness:** diffuse — no single protagonist, more macro/complex. Great number, harder to make gripping. Backup.

## The Korea gap (worth a decision)
You specifically want **Korean** frauds, but the data shows a tension: the best-scale, most
evergreen Korean story — **Daewoo Group** (~$43B, founder Kim Woo-choong inflated the books
and fled abroad for years; once the world's biggest bankruptcy) — **already has a strong 1M-view
treatment by Asianometry**. The genuinely *under-covered* Korean cases —
**Optimus Asset Management** ($1.3B, fake government-receivable funds, political ties) and
**Wakon** ($1.1B "casino-AI" crypto Ponzi) — have friction: Optimus research is Korean-language-heavy,
and Wakon is recent/crypto (hype-risk against your evergreen rule).
**Recommendation:** Daewoo is still tellable for *your* audience with a more narrative angle
despite Asianometry's version; otherwise Korea likely needs a dedicated deeper research pass.

# APPENDIX (added 2026-07-06) — broadened beyond China/Korea

Scope widened per direction: the channel home audience is EU/US, so **"foreign" = anything
non-standard-EU/US** (Middle East, Africa, South Asia, Japan, Russia, SE Asia, Latin America).
Ran the saturation probe across 18 more candidates (`reports/saturation_global.md/.json`).
This section is **additive** — nothing above is changed.

### The key pattern in the data (this should steer topic selection)
- **Middle East & Africa are the sweet spot:** big scale, dramatic, and genuinely
  **under-covered in English** (best existing videos are regional-language or small channels).
- **India is a trap despite great stories:** Indian YouTube is enormous, so Nirav Modi (8.5M),
  Sahara (10.9M), Harshad Mehta / *Scam 1992* (23.6M), Satyam (2.9M) are **already saturated**.
  Skip for now unless you have a sharply different angle.
- **The famous ones are taken:** OneCoin (BBC, 2.6M) and **1MDB (MagnatesMedia, 4.3M)** are done
  well already. Note: **MagnatesMedia is a direct genre competitor** — worth adding to the
  inspiration-tracking set alongside Logically Answered / ColdFusion.

### New top picks (best fit from the broadened set)

**A. Abraaj Group — UAE/Dubai · ~$1B · ⭐ best story on the whole board**
- Arif Naqvi built the emerging-markets private-equity darling (Gates Foundation money in his
  health fund), then misused fund cash; collapsed 2018. Arrested in London 2019, still fighting
  US extradition. "The Key Man." Elite rise-and-fall protagonist.
- **Saturation:** only a WSJ mini-doc (~116K) — no definitive long-form. **Wide open.**

**B. Steinhoff — South Africa · $7.4B · ⭐ biggest scale + recent hook**
- "Enron of South Africa." CEO **Markus Jooste died by suicide in 2024** as prosecution loomed.
  Huge number, tragic ending, global retailer (Mattress Firm, etc.) most viewers unknowingly touched.
- **Saturation:** best treatment is a SA channel (~191K) — under-covered internationally.

**C. Thodex — Turkey · ~$2B · ⭐ best "hook" title**
- Founder Faruk Fatih Özer fled with users' crypto, caught in Albania, **sentenced to 11,196 years.**
  The absurd sentence is an instant thumbnail/title.
- **Saturation:** top is a German video (~100K); English lane open.

**D. NMC Health — UAE/Abu Dhabi · $4B hidden debt**
- Migrant-pharmacist BR Shetty built a FTSE-100 hospital empire that hid billions; collapsed 2020.
  Rags-to-riches-to-ruin arc with an audit-failure spine.
- **Saturation:** best is a Malayalam video (~28K) — **English near-zero.**

**E. Mirror Trading International — South Africa · $588M BTC** *(crypto caveat)*
- Largest crypto scam of 2020 by victim count; Johann Steynberg fled to Brazil and **died in
  custody 2024.** Strong true-crime, though crypto is less evergreen than the above.
