## Notes

- **NMC Health** (UAE/Abu Dhabi, $4B hidden debt) — BR Shetty's FTSE-100 hospital empire hid billions in debt; collapsed 2020. Rags-to-riches-to-ruin migrant-tycoon story.
- **Oceanografia / Citibank** (Mexico, $400M+) — Forged invoices to Pemex used to defraud Citibank's Banamex; founder Amado Yanez jailed. Under-covered in English.
- **Abraaj Group** (UAE/Dubai, $1B+) — Arif Naqvi's emerging-markets PE giant collapsed 2018; misused a health fund. 'The Key Man'. Fugitive fighting extradition in London. Elite protagonist arc.
- **Thodex** (Turkey, $2B) — Founder fled with users' funds, caught in Albania, sentenced to 11,196 YEARS. Absurd-sentence hook, strong visuals.
- **Ciftlik Bank** (Turkey, $130M+) — Mobile-game 'virtual farm' Ponzi; founder fled to Uruguay/Brazil then surrendered. Quirky, very under-covered in English.
- **Steinhoff** (South Africa, $7.4B) — 'Enron of South Africa'; CEO Markus Jooste died by suicide in 2024 as arrest loomed. Big scale + recent tragic ending.
- **Mirror Trading International** (South Africa, $588M BTC) — Largest crypto scam of 2020 by victim count; Steynberg fled to Brazil, died in custody 2024. Under-covered.
- **Toshiba accounting** (Japan, $1.2B) — Systematic profit-padding under boardroom pressure; toppled leadership. Corporate-culture angle; moderately covered.
- **Olympus scandal** (Japan, $1.7B) — Decades-long loss-hiding exposed by CEO-whistleblower Michael Woodford, who was fired for asking questions. Great insider hook.
- **MMM / Sergei Mavrodi** (Russia, millions of victims) — The archetypal post-Soviet pyramid; up to 10M victims in 1990s Russia, later exported to Africa/Asia. Evergreen origin-of-Ponzi story.
- **Hin Leong / O.K. Lim** (Singapore, $3.5B) — Oil-trading empire hid huge losses and forged documents; collapsed 2020, Lim family convicted. Under-covered outside Asia.
- **DMG / David Murcia** (Colombia, $1B+) — Prepaid-card pyramid that gripped Colombia in 2008; founder extradited to US. Very under-covered for its national impact.
- **Satyam Computers** (India, $1.5B) — 'India's Enron'; chairman Ramalinga Raju confessed in a letter to inflating profits for years. Iconic confession hook.
- **OneCoin / Ruja Ignatova** (Bulgaria, $4B+) — 'Cryptoqueen' vanished in 2017; still FBI Most Wanted. Massive — but likely already well covered (BBC podcast/docs); test saturation.
- **1MDB / Jho Low** (Malaysia, $4.5B) — State-fund looting funding Hollywood/yachts; Jho Low still at large. Enormous — but heavily covered (books/docs); test saturation.
- **Nirav Modi / PNB** (India, $2B) — Diamond tycoon rigged fake bank guarantees (LoUs); fled to London, long extradition fight. Glamorous downfall.
- **Harshad Mehta 1992 scam** (India, $1B (1992)) — The 'Big Bull' rigged the Bombay stock market with bank receipts. Huge in India (Scam 1992) but under-told for Western audiences.
- **Sahara / Subrata Roy** (India, $3-4B) — Opaque deposit empire, 30M+ small investors, SEBI battle, chairman jailed. Massive scale, murky and under-covered abroad.
